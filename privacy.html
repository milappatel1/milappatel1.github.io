Driftglow Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Driftglow is made by Milap Patel, who publishes on Google Play as MastionGames ("we").

What we collect ourselves

  • Nothing. There are no accounts, and we run no servers or analytics of our own.
  • Your progress, settings and hint balance are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Your device's own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include them, depending on your device settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Driftglow shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device: your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before any personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad, you can allow or deny tracking, and change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • If you buy Remove Ads or the Voyager Pass, interstitial ads stop. Optional reward videos only load if you choose to watch one.

Purchases

  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. The game asks the store which items you own so it can unlock them.

Children

  • Driftglow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
