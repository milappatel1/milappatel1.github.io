Driftglow

Glide a paper boat across a quiet night pond and light every lantern.

Play free on Google Play Coming later to the App Store

Swipe to glide. Your boat keeps sliding until something stops it. Pass over a lantern to light it, and light them all to finish the puzzle. 300 puzzles, a new Daily Puzzle every day, and no timers or pressure.
